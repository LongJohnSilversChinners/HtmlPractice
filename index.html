About Me

My name is Seth Ramirez, and I am a Belizean student, youth leader, and aspiring computer engineer. As a student at Belize High School, I have always tried to challenge myself academically while also taking on leadership roles that allow me to support and inspire others. I have served as class president, prefect, and a member of the Honor Society, experiences that have helped me grow in responsibility, confidence, and discipline. One of my biggest passions is STEM, especially robotics. I am a three-time member of Team Belize for the FIRST Global Challenge, and I currently serve as team captain. Through robotics, I have learned how to design, build, troubleshoot, and work with a team under pressure. I have also participated in the FIRST Lego League, where I earned gold and silver awards. These experiences have strengthened my love for engineering and have helped shape my goal of pursuing computer engineering in the future. Beyond robotics, I have also taken part in math and coding competitions, spelling bee competitions, and international leadership opportunities. Each experience has taught me something valuable, whether it was how to communicate better, solve problems creatively, or stay committed even when things become difficult. I believe strongly in continuous improvement and the idea that small, consistent steps can lead to major growth. Whether I am leading a team, mentoring younger students, working on a project, or preparing for college, I try to keep improving every day. My goal is to use technology and engineering to create solutions that can help my community, represent Belize proudly, and open doors for future students like me.
